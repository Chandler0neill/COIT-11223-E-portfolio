# Summary of the artefact
This artefact explores how Artificial intelligence is being wrongfully utilised in Australian government. The article highlights how AI can falsely cite sources and create content that doesn't even exist due to Ai hallucinations. It describes how the cycle of misinformation can create difficulties when fact checking. The guardian states that whilst creating this article, at least 39 political inquiries contained what appeared to be hallucinated references. Overall it raises concerns in regard to government officials using Artificial intelligence to make important decisions and questions the reliability of them when doing so.   

# Justification on why I chose this artefact
I chose this artefact because I believe it is a good negative example of AI. I found it interesting that government officials are utilising Artificial intelligence for such important and public facing decisions. Furthermore, it highlighted the importance of fact checking the information that is uploaded to the internet. If the government is uploading fabricated information, it shows that anyone can. This connects to ICT ethics because technology professionals have a responsibility to develop and use AI systems transparently, accurately and ethically.
